Intelligent ACA software

Automatic 1095-C coding. Complex reporting, simplified.

Company settings, employee records and coverage details work together to prepare monthly codes, contribution amounts and filing-ready forms—reducing hours of manual review.

INTELLIGENT DATA IMPORT

Start with your Excel reports

Read raw employee demographics, payroll hours, and insurance census reports. The intelligent import tool identifies and imports the information needed for ACA reporting.

AUTOMATIC CODE ASSIGNMENT

1095 software with automatic coding

The software analyzes employee and insurance data to determine and apply the appropriate monthly codes and Line 15 amounts, including reporting for Lines 14, 15, and 16.

REVIEW AND VALIDATION

Resolve exceptions before filing

Diagnostics identify missing or invalid SSNs, incomplete addresses, duplicate records, missing hours and coverage-date conflicts. Correct flagged data, then review employee forms and employer totals before submission.

ELECTRONIC FILING

Federal and applicable state returns

Prepare Forms 1094-C and 1095-C, authorize filing and track acknowledgements. State services include CA, NJ, DC, RI and Massachusetts 1099-HC reporting. State filing is a DIY add-on, with one applicable state included in Managed Filing.

COMPLEX REPORTING

Self-insured plans, ICHRA and complex coverage

Manage self-insurance reporting for self-insured and self-funded plans, covered individuals, and individual coverage health reimbursement arrangements (ICHRA). Apply monthly 1095-C codes as eligibility and coverage change, with targeted review for COBRA and union arrangements.

EMPLOYEE FORM ACCESS

Online document retrieval

Generate 1095-C PDFs and provide secure employee access through a dedicated online retrieval portal.
