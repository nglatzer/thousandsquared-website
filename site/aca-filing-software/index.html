More than form entry

ACA reporting software that does the detailed coding work.

Assigning codes employee by employee and month by month can take significant staff time. ThousandSquared combines company settings with employee and coverage data to prepare the reporting results automatically.

DATA PREPARATION

Intelligent Excel imports

Work with raw employee demographics, payroll hours, and insurance census reports. The import tools identify relevant information so your team can review the imported records and address missing or inconsistent data.

AUTOMATIC CODING

Monthly 1095-C results

The software uses employee information, coverage details, and company questionnaire settings to apply monthly codes and contribution amounts. Review the results and investigate exceptions before finalizing your forms.

EXCEPTION REVIEW

Actionable diagnostics

Find issues such as missing or invalid SSNs, incomplete addresses, duplicate records, coverage-date conflicts, missing hours, and incomplete special-program settings. Correct the source information and review the updated results.

FORM PREPARATION

1094-C and 1095-C review

Review employee identity and monthly reporting on Form 1095-C, then confirm company information and employer totals on Form 1094-C. Employee PDFs and filing files are part of the connected preparation workflow.

COMPLEX COVERAGE

Self-insured plans and ICHRA

Handle self-insured covered-individual information and ICHRA-related reporting in the same workflow. COBRA, union arrangements, and changing employment or coverage circumstances receive targeted review where needed.

FILING SERVICES

Federal and applicable states

Prepare for IRS filing and applicable state submissions. State services include CA, NJ, DC, RI, and Massachusetts 1099-HC reporting. Confirm required registrations, authorizations, and submission arrangements with our team.

Prepare once. Review carefully.

What your company needs to get started.

A brief company questionnaire establishes the reporting context. Accurate source reports give the software the information needed to apply the coding rules to your employees.

Read the Data Preparation FAQ

Your preparation checklist

  • Company legal name, EIN, address, contact, and reporting year.
  • Employee demographics, employment dates, and classifications.
  • Payroll hours and relevant measurement-period information.
  • Coverage offers, enrollment dates, and required employee contributions.
  • Covered individuals for self-insured plans and details of special arrangements.

Your company remains responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information supplied. Provide sensitive records through the approved secure application workflow.

Review before submission

Diagnostics turn missing information into a clear review task.

Use the Diagnostics screen to prioritize issues by category, severity, and status. Investigate the underlying record, correct the data or setting, update diagnostics, and review the affected forms again.
